About the Olympics

The Olympic Games are the world's foremost sports competition, bringing together athletes from more than 200 nations. Los Angeles 2028 will mark the return of the Games to the United States, celebrating unity, excellence, and the human spirit.

With state-of-the-art venues and a commitment to sustainability, LA 2028 promises to be a landmark event for athletes and fans alike.
